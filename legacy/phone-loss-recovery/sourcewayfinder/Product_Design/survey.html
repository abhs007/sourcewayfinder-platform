Allow about 5 minutes. This voluntary survey is for adults aged 18 or older. Easy experiences and difficult experiences are equally useful. Most questions are optional.

How your answers will be used

Abhishek Vaishnav is collecting feedback to guide this independent project. Responses may be summarized in project reports, research, and supporting documentation describing the project. This survey does not test the tool’s accuracy.

We do not ask for your name, email, exact address, or account details. Please do not include these in comments. The project owner controls access to the response database. Hosting providers process data to operate the service. Responses are stored when you choose Submit. You can leave before submitting. Because no identity is requested, we may not be able to identify and withdraw your response afterward.

On the public website, responses are stored in Cloudflare D1. The application does not store IP addresses or browser identifiers with your answers. Cloudflare processes connection information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the service. We use brief IP-based rate limits to reduce automated submissions. The application sets no analytics cookies and does not log response contents. A daily cleanup removes responses older than 180 days from the active database; provider backups may persist under their own retention rules. Locally saved exports must be managed separately by the project owner.

For project or privacy questions, contact the project owner through GitHub. GitHub issues are public; do not post your survey answers or personal information there.
