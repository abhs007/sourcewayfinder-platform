# Fictional-task feasibility protocol

This is study preparation, not recruitment, ethics approval or a completed study. Before recruiting, finalize applicable review, consent, privacy and retention arrangements. A possible initial feasibility cohort is six to ten consenting adults; small samples do not establish population effectiveness.

Twelve manually authored tasks provide variants A/B for each provider. A session has six tasks, one per provider. Schedules 1/2 use A, 3/4 use B. Odd schedules assign the first three providers to navigator and the others to official links; even schedules reverse this. Rotate the four schedules and record actual allocation. Seeded ordering is reproducible. Variants can differ in difficulty: counterbalancing does not establish equivalence.

Official-link tasks show the cited pages; navigator tasks show explained guidance. Participants answer locally. Wall-clock elapsed time includes external reading and interruptions, not measured active reading time. Feedback waits until completion. Help requests are counts. Exports contain random session ID, seed/schedule, catalog version, task IDs/conditions, answers, correctness, elapsed milliseconds and help counts. No name, account secret, transcript or automatic upload is requested.

Use synthetic mode for development. For an actual appropriately reviewed session, explain the instrument and obtain applicable consent before deselecting synthetic. Do not coach, request real account demonstrations or change a working line. Honor withdrawal. Delete the tab session; downloaded exports require separate management under the adopted protocol. Random IDs alone do not guarantee anonymity when linked to outside records.

Task rubrics in content/tasks.json are manually authored, not generated by the engine. Have an outside qualified reader review cited interpretations before relying on comparative participant results; record disagreement and corrections. Report actual sample, allocation, task difficulty/order, help, times, omissions and limitations. No synthetic run is a human result or real recovery measurement.
